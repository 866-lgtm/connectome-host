- A Web UI observer is no longer sent a message whose every part its grant
  leaves out, such as a tool's call and result without `tools` or a turn of
  thinking alone without `thinking`, which reached it as an empty row with
  its time. A page of older history that such messages would leave empty
  reads further back, up to ten pages' worth, so a page comes back empty
  only at the start of history or after ten pages' worth of such messages,
  where the "older messages" row still offers more. An observer that
  connects while such messages fill the welcome's tail opens on the last
  messages it may see, read back the same way.
