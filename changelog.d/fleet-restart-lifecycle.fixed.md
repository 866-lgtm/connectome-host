- Fleet manual restarts preserve environment overrides, autoRestart, and subscriptions. Failed socket setup, subscription, or readiness now terminates the owned child before returning failure; unconfirmed termination stays blocked and is reported explicitly. The record's `exitReason` keeps the launch failure beside the exit, whichever came first.
- Fleet automatic replacements retain crash history, advance the existing backoff, and stop after three retries within 60 seconds, where a crash-looping child used to be replaced forever. `fleet--status` shows the stop as `autoRestartStopped`. A manual restart, or a launch of the same name, resets that retry budget, and kill, restart, launch or shutdown cancels a pending automatic replacement; a manual restart or launch also cancels one already under way. A replacement that fails to start ends automatic restarts until a manual restart or launch, as before.
- Fleet launches, manual and automatic, are refused without spawning when, during their awaits, the parent begins shutting down, a kill is acknowledged on the generation they replace, or another launch replaces it. The refusal names which.
- Fleet restarts also work for adopted children after confirmed shutdown. Startup tolerates transient stale-socket refusals while the replacement binds, and reports a child that died after binding its socket with the refused connect. A natural nonzero exit racing failed-launch cleanup retains its automatic retry.
